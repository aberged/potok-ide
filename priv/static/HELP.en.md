# How to use Potok

Everything you need to get going: how Potok works, how to organize your community in it and where to find things in the app. The pictures come from the made-up neighbourhood of Topolik and its neighbours.

## First steps {:#prvi-koraci}

### Getting in {:#ulazak}

For now, you join Potok by invitation. Someone who is already here enters your email, and you get a message with a link that is valid for seven days. Once you open it, the account is yours: set a password in the settings, or log in each time with a magic link that comes to your email and is valid for fifteen minutes.

On the log-in page you enter your email and password. If you turn on "Keep me logged in on this device", Potok remembers you for two weeks.

![Logging in with a password; the link below it logs in with a magic link](prijava)

### On your phone and your computer {:#telefon}

Potok runs in the browser, on a computer and on a phone, and there is an app for your phone too. You can also install it straight from the browser, and it then opens like an app. It is the same account everywhere, with the same groups and messages.

### Finding your way {:#snalazenje}

The top bar is always the same. The house leads to the home page and counts your unread messages. The bell leads to the notices of the groups you follow, # to tags, and the two icons right of it to invitations and access requests. Your picture leads to your profiles.

The button with three lines opens the menu: every part of the app, your account settings, the language (Serbian in Latin or Cyrillic, or English), the light or dark theme, this help and what Potok costs.

![The home page: groups, with their unread messages and newest message](pocetna) ![The menu with every part of the app](meni)

## How Potok works {:#kako-radi}

Potok is made of a few simple parts that fit inside one another. Once you know them, any community can be put together in it the way it really works.

### Account and profiles {:#nalog-i-profili}

Your account is you: the email and password you log in with. In groups, others see you as a profile, with a name and a picture. One account can have several profiles, say a personal one and one for an association, and you choose which one you are using.

A profile is unique or shared. A shared profile is used by several accounts together, so a building's council or a family speaks with one voice, while everyone logs in with their own account.

### Groups in a tree {:#grupe}

Everything happens in groups, and every group can have sub-groups of its own, as deep as needed. The home page is at the top, and under it are the groups you are in. A local community thus has a group for notices, a group for its assembly and a map of the neighbourhood, and the map has a group for each problem in the neighbourhood.

A one-to-one conversation is a group too, with just two members. A thread, a longer conversation opened from one message, is a group under the one where the message was written.

![Sub-groups as folders: the neighbourhood map open, with a group for each problem](podgrupe-folderi)

### What a group shares {:#sadrzaj}

In a group, people write messages, send files, plan events and start polls, and a group can have a description: a text, a page or a small app. All of it stays in the group, and those who may see the group see it.

### Membership and inheritance {:#clanstvo}

You get into a group by invitation, or by a request that someone approves. A sub-group can inherit members: whoever is a member of the group above is a member of it too, with the same roles. So the assembly and the neighbourhood map have all the local community's members at once, and whoever is no longer a member of the community is no longer theirs.

### Roles and permissions {:#uloge-i-dozvole}

What each person may do in a group is set by roles. Every group has the role Admin, which may do everything, and the role Everyone, which every member has. You can add roles of your own too, say council or moderators. For each of the 20 permissions, a role says "Allow", "Deny" or "Not set", and a denial is stronger than an allowance.

A sub-group takes its roles from the group above. If it needs different ones, it gets a manifest of its own, its own list of roles and permissions.

### Public, private and open {:#vidljivost}

A private group is seen only by its members. A public group is seen by everyone who is logged in, who can request access, and its name, picture and a short part of its description can be seen outside Potok too, by anyone who has the link.

A public group can also open its whole description to people without an account: Edit group, Access, "Description open to guests". Whoever opens its link then reads the description, and the alternative descriptions marked "Guests read it too", and an app in the description, such as Feed or Map, only reads for them: it shows the public groups below it, but does not write, vote or react. The chat, the members and who reacted stay behind the login, and search engines do not list the page.

![A public group as someone without an account sees it](gost-opis)

A public group can also open its chat: then everyone who is logged in reads the messages and writes, without joining. Such a visitor sends no files and opens no threads.

### Following and notices {:#pracenje-i-obavestenja}

You can follow a group you see even when you are not its member. Its notices then come to you: short news the group sends to its followers, apart from the messages and with their own counter. Your phone also gets notifications about messages, mentions, events and polls, if you turn them on.

### Apps in a group {:#aplikacije-ukratko}

A group's description doesn't have to be just text. It can be a small app, say a form, news or a map, which keeps its data in the group itself and keeps to the group's permissions. You get a few such apps ready-made when you create a group.

## Examples of organizing {:#primeri}

Three communities, each put together as a tree of groups. You don't have to copy any of them: take what suits you and change it as the community grows. Groups can always be rearranged, and roles added to. Each one also has a page of its own that shows, step by step, how to put it together.

### A local community's assembly {:#primer-mz}

The local community of Topolik is a public group with its area on the map, so even those who are not members yet can see it.

- **MZ Topolik**, a folder, public, with an area on the map
  - **Council notices**: an announcements group in which the council writes, and the others read and react
  - **Citizens' assembly**: a chat, polls and the assembly's event in the calendar
  - **Neighbourhood map**: a map on which each problem is a group with its own pin and chat

The group has a manifest of its own and the role council, which may send notices to followers, publish events and manage polls. The assembly and the map inherit members, so nobody invites anyone to them separately.

A week looks like this: a neighbour puts the pothole in her street on the map, with a picture. The assembly talks about it, and the longer discussion moves to a thread. Before the assembly, the council starts a poll with a quorum and schedules the assembly as a public event at the community hall. Whoever follows the community gets a notice on their phone too.

[Step by step: how to put together a local community](/pomoc/mesna-zajednica)

![MZ Topolik: council notices, the assembly and the neighbourhood map](primer-mz) ![The neighbourhood map: problems as pins and a path along the canal](primer-mz-mapa)

### A co-op buying together {:#primer-zadruga}

- **Zadruga Livada**, a folder, private, with the pick-up place on the map
  - **Notices to buyers**: what there is this week and until when to order
  - **Orders**: a form each member fills in once and changes until the deadline
  - **Arrangements**: a chat about picking up and swaps

The pick-up is an event that repeats every Saturday, with a reminder. In the form, the organizer sees every order and a summary of them, and takes them away as a table. A dedicated app in the group's description can also run the buying rounds, the driver and the split of costs.

[Step by step: how to put together a co-op](/pomoc/zadruga)

![Zadruga Livada: notices, orders and arrangements](primer-zadruga)

### A building or a club {:#primer-zgrada}

For a small community, one chat group is enough.

- **Zgrada Cvetna 12**, a chat, private
  - **House council**: a chat for the council only

The chat holds the minutes and the house rules (all of them are also in the Files tab), a poll on the colour of the entrance and an event for cleaning the basement. The house council answers with a shared profile, so the residents know who writes on the council's behalf. A club, a choir or a sports team is put together the same way.

[Step by step: how to put together a building or a club](/pomoc/zgrada)

![Zgrada Cvetna 12: minutes, a poll and an event in the same chat](primer-zgrada)

## Profiles {:#profili}

On the Profiles page you see your current and default profile and a list of all your profiles. Tapping a profile in the list changes the one you are using, and "Create profile" makes a new one.

A username has 2 to 16 letters, digits and the characters `_`, `.` and `-`. A profile picture is resized to 200 × 200, and files over 5 MB are rejected. A profile also has a description and public tags.

You share a shared profile with another profile: in the Sharing part you type its username. The invitation shows on the Profiles page while the invited profile is the current one, and once it is accepted, both accounts use the shared profile.

![Profiles: current, default and the shared profile savet-cvetna](profili) ![Editing a profile: name, picture, description, sharing and tags](profil-uredi)

## Home and finding groups {:#pocetna}

The home page has two tabs: Conversations, with your one-to-one conversations, and Groups. Each group shows its unread messages, its newest message, its numbers of members and sub-groups, and its tags. A lock means the group is private, and a globe that it is public.

Above the list are the filters: public groups you are not a member of, threads, groups you are a member of, created or follow, the kind of group and a search by name. The groups you follow stay on the list even when you hide public groups. The search finds a group even when you type without diacritics, in Latin or in Cyrillic. Groups can be shown as a list or as folders.

The home page also has My calendar, with the events of all your groups, the Map of groups and My polls.

![The filter by kind of group](pocetna-filteri)

## A group's page {:#grupa}

At the top of a group are its name, the path to it and the tabs: Messages, Files, Calendar, Polls, Notices, Sub-groups and Members. Polls appear with the first poll, and when there is no room for all of them, Notices are in the group's menu. On the right are the bell for following, the message search and the group's menu.

![A group's header with its tabs](grupa-zaglavlje)

The group's menu has the group's description, sharing a public group's link, tags, notices, roles, creating a sub-group, inviting and editing the group. Which of these you see depends on your permissions.

A group's description can be a text, a page from a template or an app. Under it are the reactions to the group and the group's place on the map.

![The group's menu](grupa-meni) ![A group's description, reactions and area on the map](o-grupi)

## Messages {:#poruke}

You write a message in the field at the bottom and send it with the arrow. On a phone, while you type, the top bars step aside, so that as many messages as possible show.

![A chat: a reply with a quote, a thread, reactions and a private tag](poruke)

- **Reply**: a message with a quote of what you answer. Tapping the quote jumps to the original message.
- **Thread**: a longer conversation about one message, so that it doesn't bury the main chat. Under the message it says how many unread messages the thread has.
- **Reactions**: one emoji on a message. Tapping a reaction adds or removes your own.
- **Mentions**: type @ and the name of a member or a role, and @everyone mentions everyone. The message stands out for whoever is mentioned, and they get a notification.
- **Editing and deleting**: you can edit and delete your own messages, and whoever has the permission deletes other people's too.
- **Links** get a preview, and long messages fold until you open them.

On a phone, press and hold a message: a sheet opens with quick reactions and every action. On a computer, the same actions appear when you move the mouse over a message.

![Pressing and holding a message](poruka-meni) ![A reply: the quote is above the field you write in](poruke-odgovor) ![A thread opened from a message](nit)

The + button next to the field adds files or starts a poll. One message carries up to 10 files, up to 100 MB each. Pictures, video and sound open right in the chat, and you can also drag files into the window. In the Android app, you send a GIF or a sticker straight from the keyboard.

When you come back to a chat, a line shows where the unread messages begin. The magnifier in the header searches the group's messages, in threads too, and even when you type without diacritics, in Latin or in Cyrillic.

![Files and a poll under the + button](prilozi) ![Searching messages](pretraga-poruka) ![A line shows where the unread messages begin](neprocitano)

## New groups and settings {:#nove-grupe}

You create a sub-group with the + button on the Sub-groups tab or from the group's menu. First you pick what kind of group you need:

- **Chat**: opens on its messages, and all members can write.
- **Folder**: keeps sub-groups together.
- **Announcements**: only administrators write, the others read and react.
- **Feed**: news you swipe through, with a part where its editors write it.
- **Polls**: all of the group's polls in one place.
- **Form**: questions, answers and a summary of them.
- **Map**: sub-groups as pins on a map. It is not offered on the home page, only inside a group.
- **Page**: a text people see first, made from a template.

In the second step you enter the name and choose whether the group is public and whether it inherits members. "Set everything up yourself" opens the full form.

![What kind of group do you need?](kreiraj-podgrupu) ![Name, public or not, and inheriting members](kreiraj-korak)

Edit group has everything else: name and picture, access and membership (public group, description open to guests, chat open to non-members, inheritance), what opens first, the description, alternative descriptions, the location and the danger zone for deleting. For a public group, you also see there how a link to it looks when it is shared outside Potok.

![Edit group: access and membership, with how the link looks outside Potok](uredi-grupu)

## Members and roles {:#clanovi}

The Members tab shows all members, their roles and who is online. From there, access requests are approved, roles given, and members removed and blocked. Inherited members are marked, and tapping a member opens a one-to-one conversation.

You can invite a profile by username, or someone by email: if they have no account yet, they get an invitation to Potok.

![Members and access requests](clanovi) ![Inviting into a group](pozovi)

In Roles you see whether the group has a manifest of its own or inherits one, you add roles of your own and, for each permission, choose "Allow", "Not set" or "Deny". The role Everyone can only allow.

![Roles and their permissions](uloge)

## Calendar {:#kalendar}

A group's calendar shows a month or a list of events, and it can include sub-groups. My calendar on the home page gathers the events of all your groups and, if you want, public events.

An event can be one-off, all-day or repeat every day, week, month or year. You give it a place in words, as a group that stands on the map, or draw it on the map as a point, an area or a route. With an event come reminders for members, an announcement in the chat and a notification if the time or place changes.

You answer an event with Going, Maybe or Not going, and see who is coming. "Add to my calendar" takes it into a Google, Apple or Outlook calendar. A public event is seen outside the group too.

![My calendar](kalendar) ![An event: time, place, who is coming and reminders](dogadjaj) ![A new event](dogadjaj-forma)

## Polls {:#glasanja}

You start a poll from the chat (the + button, then New poll) or on the Polls tab. You enter the question and answers, or pick the ready-made "Yes / No" and "For / Against / Abstain", and a deadline.

Under "More settings" are:

- one answer or several, and how many at most;
- a secret poll, changing your vote and answers added by members;
- a public poll, in which non-members vote too;
- who votes, who sees the results and who sees who voted for what;
- the decision rule (the most votes, more than half of the votes, two thirds of the votes or more than half of those who may vote) and a quorum.

![A new poll](glasanje-forma) ![A poll in the chat, after voting](glasanje-kartica)

You vote right from the chat. The Polls tab shows the open polls, those waiting for your vote and the finished ones, and their details show the turnout, who voted for what (if that may be seen), who has not voted and the results to download.

In a secret poll nobody, not an admin and not an app, sees who voted for what. The numbers don't show before the end, and a finished secret poll cannot be opened again.

![A poll's details: turnout and who voted for what](glasanje-detalji)

## Map and places {:#mapa}

The map of groups on the home page shows every group with a place that you may see. Groups close together gather into a circle with a number. You narrow it with the same filters as the list, plus time and pin, and its layers turn places, areas and routes on and off. Tapping a pin opens the group's card.

![The map of groups, with the local community's area](mapa-grupa) ![A group's card on the map](mapa-kartica)

You give a group its place in Edit group, under Location:

- **Point**: a tap on the map, or your location.
- **Area**: a border you draw with its corners, like a local community's, or bring from a GeoJSON file. The map then shows what lies in the area.
- **Route**: a walk, a race or a bike path, drawn or brought from a GPX, KML or GeoJSON file. The map also shows which areas it passes through.

For the pin you choose one of 33 icons or the group's picture, and the pin's corner shows the group's kind. A group with a map (Map) shows its sub-groups as pins, and "Add a place" makes a new one.

![Location: the local community's area](lokacija-podrucje)

## Files and tags {:#fajlovi-i-tagovi}

The Files tab gathers all files from the group's messages, as a list or in folders of sub-groups, and from each file you can go to the message it came with. Only members download files, apart from pictures in a group's description and files from an open chat.

You put a tag on groups, profiles, events, messages and files, with the # button. A public tag is seen by everyone who sees the thing it is on, and a private one, with a lock, only by you. One thing carries up to 10 public and 10 private tags. On the Tags page you choose one or more tags and see everything that carries them.

![A group's files](fajlovi) ![Tags: private and public](tagovi)

## Following, notices and push {:#obavestenja}

The bell in a group's header is for following. One tap and you follow the group. After that, the bell opens the choices: push notifications for this group, the Notices tab and stopping following. The home page, one-to-one conversations and threads cannot be followed.

Whoever has the permission sends a notice to all followers on the Notices tab, up to 2000 characters. The Notices page (the bell in the top bar) has the notices you received and a list of the groups you follow. On the home page and on the map of groups, the filter "Groups I follow" leaves only them.

![Following a group](pracenje) ![Notices received](obavestenja)

You turn on push notifications in your account settings, separately for each browser or phone. They come for new messages, mentions, replies and reactions, invitations and approved requests, events and reminders, polls and form responses. There is also a separate switch for the notices of the groups you follow.

![Settings: push notifications](podesavanja-push)

## Apps in a group {:#aplikacije}

Some kinds of groups get a ready-made app:

- **Feed** is a row of news you swipe through, with reactions and comments. Its editors write them in the sub-group `.feed`, from templates, keep them as drafts and publish them. For people without an account to read the news too, turn on "Description open to guests" both in the Feed and in `.feed`: a new story takes that choice from `.feed`.
- **Form** has questions of many kinds, rules (who fills it in, once or more often, until when, anonymously), a summary of the answers, a quiz and attached files.
- **Polls** shows all of the group's polls in one place.
- **Map** shows the sub-groups as pins, areas and routes.

![Feed: news from the neighbourhood](feed) ![The order form, as a member sees it](forma)

You can write any group's description from a template: news, photo, quote, event, list, link, notice, question or free text. A group can also have up to 20 alternative descriptions, each of which only certain roles may see, or visitors without an account too, once the group opens its description to them.

Whoever writes HTML can turn a description into an app of their own (Edit group, Description, "The description is an app (API)"). What an app may do, and what it may not, is described in the [domain map](/domain#domain-apps).

![Templates for a group's description](opis-sablon)

### Sharing into Potok {:#deljenje}

You share a text, a link or a file from another app on your phone into Potok. You choose a group or a conversation, and what you shared waits for you in the field you write in. Potok installed from the browser does the same.

You share a link to a public group or a public event from the group's menu or from the event. Outside Potok, the name, picture and a short part of the description can be seen, and the whole description once the group opens it to visitors without an account.
